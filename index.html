The Challenge

LoopNote needed a paid social strategy to drive paid trial starts below $90 CPA on Meta and TikTok — in a crowded AI notetaking market dominated by Otter.ai, Fathom, and Granola.

My Approach

Mapped 5 competitors and 8 real benchmark ads. Read performance data from 10 past creatives to find the best-performing format ($62 CPA, screen-record on TikTok). Developed a creative brief, 3 original ad concepts, and a $9,000 two-week test plan.

"Every competitor lives inside the meeting room. Nobody owns the shower idea, the run thought, the 2am voice memo. That uncontested emotional territory became LoopNote's entire campaign direction."

Clinical Data Analysis · UCLA

MIMIC-IV ICU Data Analysis

Academic Project · Jan – Apr 2026 · University of California, Los Angeles

↗ View R Shiny Dashboard on GitHub
SQL R · R Shiny Statistical Modeling Logistic Regression ggplot2
The Challenge

Investigate hemodynamic outcome variability in ICU patients using the MIMIC-IV clinical database — one of the largest freely-available critical care datasets. Goal: identify significant predictors of MAP trends and ICU outcomes to support clinical decision-making.

What I Did

Extracted and queried 50,000+ ICU patient records using SQL. Applied logistic regression and mixed-effects models. Built an interactive R Shiny dashboard with ggplot2 visualizations — enabling dynamic exploration of patient vitals and clinical outcomes for stakeholders.

Key Finding

"Identified three significant predictor variables for ICU outcome variability. The R Shiny dashboard translated 50K+ raw clinical records into an interactive tool that clinical stakeholders could actually use — the same translation challenge I apply to marketing data."
