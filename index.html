LoopNote needed a paid social strategy to drive paid trial starts below $90 CPA on Meta and TikTok — in a crowded AI notetaking market dominated by Otter.ai, Fathom, and Granola.

My Approach

MIMIC-IV ICU Data Analysis

Academic Project · Jan – Apr 2026 · University of California, Los Angeles

SQLR · R Shiny Logistic RegressionMixed-Effects Modelsggplot2
The Challenge

Investigate hemodynamic outcome variability in ICU patients using the MIMIC-IV clinical database — one of the largest freely-available critical care datasets. Identify significant predictors of MAP trends and ICU outcomes.

What I Did

Extracted and queried 50,000+ ICU patient records using SQL. Applied logistic regression and mixed-effects models. Built an interactive R Shiny dashboard with ggplot2 visualizations for dynamic exploration of patient vitals and clinical outcomes.

Key Finding

"Identified three significant predictor variables for ICU outcome variability. The R Shiny dashboard translated 50K+ raw clinical records into a tool stakeholders could actually use — the same translation challenge I apply to marketing data."
