LoopNote AI — Growth Strategy

Full creative strategy for an AI voice-notes app · Meta + TikTok · 2025

Competitor Analysis Creative Research Data Analysis Ad Concepts Test Plan
The Challenge

LoopNote needed a paid social strategy to drive paid trial starts below $90 CPA on Meta and TikTok — in a crowded AI notetaking market dominated by Otter.ai, Fathom, and Granola.

My Approach

Analyzed 5 competitors and 8 real benchmark ads across platforms. Read performance data from 10 past creatives to find the lowest cost-per-paid-start format ($62, screen-record demo on TikTok). Developed a creative brief, 3 original ad concepts, and a 2-week $9,000 test plan.

Core Insight

"Every competitor lives inside the meeting room. Nobody owns the shower idea, the run thought, the 2am voice memo. That uncontested emotional territory became LoopNote's entire campaign direction."
