The Challenge

Investigate hemodynamic outcome variability in ICU patients using the MIMIC-IV clinical database. Identify significant predictors of MAP trends to support clinical decision-making.

What I Did

Queried and cleaned 50,000+ ICU patient records via SQL. Built an analytical cohort with 15+ engineered features. Applied logistic regression and mixed-effects models to identify 3 significant hemodynamic predictors (p<0.05).

Key Finding

"The R Shiny dashboard translated 50K+ raw clinical records into a tool stakeholders could actually use — the same translation challenge I apply to business data every day."
